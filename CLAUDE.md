\## Problem Solving Principles



\- When encountering warnings, errors, or convergence issues, NEVER just suppress or silence them. Always investigate and fix the root cause first. Only suppress after confirming the underlying issue is resolved and the warning is truly benign.

\- When asked to fix something, do NOT take the lazy path (suppressing, commenting out, or wrapping in try/except). Fix the actual problem. If unsure of the root cause, investigate first and present options before making changes.
